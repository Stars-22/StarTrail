inkdown is a local-first Markdown editor: every document lives in IndexedDB. No account, no network required, and your files can leave whenever you want.

The editing surface is deeply customized on ProseMirror, with synced split view, LaTeX and Mermaid rendering.

## Highlights

- Fully offline, installable as a PWA
- Synced split view + typewriter mode
- LaTeX math and Mermaid diagrams
