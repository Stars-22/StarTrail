readflow is an anti-algorithm RSS reader: once a day at a fixed time it delivers a digest of your feeds, and stays silent the rest of the day.

It was my first paid product, with 300+ subscribers, and took me through the full loop of building, shipping and getting paid for the first time.

## Highlights

- Once-a-day "slow push" digest
- Full-text cache and read-later
- Search and keyboard-first navigation
