Twelve generation and four solving algorithms implemented in Rust, with Criterion benchmarks and property-based tests.

It started as a Rust learning project, and turned out to be the fastest way to understand ownership — the harder the compiler scolds, the deeper the memory.

## Highlights

- 12 generation + 4 solving algorithms
- Criterion benchmarks covering every algorithm
- Exports both PNG and ASCII renderings
