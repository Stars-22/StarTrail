A pixel-art workbench in the browser, supporting palette constraints, onion skinning and frame-by-frame animation export.

No framework at all — a hand-written Canvas + TypeScript rendering pipeline, kept under 90kb total.

## Highlights

- Palette-constraint mode
- Onion skin + frame animation
- Exports PNG / GIF / sprite sheets
